Privacy Policy

How the Three Good Things app and this website handle information.

Publisher · Three Good Things Developer

Contact · threegoodthings.support@gmail.com

Effective date · November 8, 2026

Last updated · November 9, 2026

Journal entries, photos and settings

The app stores entries, drafts, attached photos and preferences, including notification settings, in its storage on your device. The app has no account or publisher-operated journal server. It does not include journal text or attached photos in advertising requests or reminders. It uses system permissions and photo selection features when you attach or take a photo.

Backup and restore

When you export a backup, it contains saved entries and available attached photos. The backup is unencrypted and may contain location or other metadata present in the attached photos. The location you choose, such as iCloud Drive, may sync the file under that provider’s terms. The app validates backups on your device and keeps existing entries and drafts when dates conflict. You control where exported files are kept and how they are deleted.

Voice input

When you start voice input, the app uses your microphone and the device’s speech recognition service. Depending on your device, system, language and settings, the service provider, such as Apple, Google or another service you have selected, may receive audio and information needed to transcribe it. The transcription goes into the journal editor. The publisher does not operate an audio storage service. You can turn off microphone access in your device settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

Versions with advertising configured use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and, where configured, advertising technology providers may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and device information, available identifiers, privacy choices, ad interactions and performance or diagnostic data. This information may be used to determine which privacy message to show, deliver, personalize and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain service quality. Actual processing depends on the platform, region, your choices and the advertising configuration.

The app may use personalized ads where permitted by applicable law, your region, your privacy choices and system permissions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, UMP collects choices about advertising purposes and providers. Declining consent or allowing only some purposes or providers is not treated by the app as consent to all of them. In applicable US regions, UMP provides choices to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Google’s advertising SDK uses the privacy signals supplied by UMP to determine the applicable advertising treatment.

Depending on those choices and the advertising configuration, non-personalized or limited ads may be served, or no ads may be shown. These ads may still involve information such as IP addresses to deliver ads, permitted measurement, and fraud prevention. Depending on Google’s limited-ad settings, fraud-prevention cookies or local storage may also be used. Declining personalization does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

Where available and permitted, the Android advertising ID and app- or publisher-scoped identifiers may be used. On iOS, access to Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and tracking as defined by Apple require App Tracking Transparency permission. Where applicable, UMP presents the permission flow. Google’s SDK does not send IDFA in ad requests when ATT permission is denied. ATT permission does not replace regional consent or override opt-outs. The app does not send journal text or attached photos for advertising use.

When UMP provides the option, go to Settings → Privacy settings to review, change or withdraw advertising choices, including applicable US opt-outs. You can change available iOS tracking permissions in system Settings. The journal remains usable regardless of these choices.

This website

These pages do not use advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, including when visitors are signed out. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Journal entries are kept on your device until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. You can delete entries from History. Uninstalling the app normally removes local app data. Delete exported backups separately from the device or storage service where you saved them.

If you email the publisher, the publisher receives your email address and the information you include in the message to respond to your inquiry. Use the address above for privacy requests, including access, correction or deletion where applicable. Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of your data or complain to a privacy regulator. For information handled by a third-party service, see that provider’s information about available controls.
