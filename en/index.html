Privacy Policy

How the Three Good Things app and this website handle information.

Review draft. Prepared November 8, 2026. The publisher’s name, public contact and effective date are pending confirmation.

Journal entries, photos and settings

The app stores entries, drafts, attached photos and preferences, including notification settings, in its storage on your device. The app has no account or publisher-operated journal server. It does not include journal text or attached photos in advertising requests or reminders. It uses system permissions and photo selection features when you attach or take a photo.

Backup and restore

When you export a backup, it contains saved entries and available attached photos. The backup is unencrypted and may contain location or other metadata present in the attached photos. The location you choose, such as iCloud Drive, may sync the file under that provider’s terms. The app validates backups on your device and keeps existing entries and drafts when dates conflict. You control where exported files are kept and how they are deleted.

Voice input

When you start voice input, the app uses your microphone and the device’s speech recognition service. Depending on your device, system, language and settings, the service provider, such as Apple, Google or another service you have selected, may receive audio and information needed to transcribe it. The transcription goes into the journal editor. The publisher does not operate an audio storage service. You can turn off microphone access in your device settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

Versions with advertising configured use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and, where configured, advertising technology providers may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and device information, available identifiers, privacy choices, ad interactions and performance or diagnostic data. This information may be used to determine which privacy message to show, deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain service quality. Actual processing depends on the platform, region, your choices and the advertising configuration.

The app requests non-personalized ads. Those ads can still use some data or identifiers for purposes such as measuring ads and preventing fraud, subject to the applicable settings and permissions. The Android distribution configuration removes permissions for the Android advertising ID. Other device or app identifiers may still be processed. Any use of Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) requires system permission.

When UMP makes the option available, go to Settings → Privacy settings to review, change or withdraw your advertising choices. If iOS offers a tracking setting for this app, you can change it in system Settings. The journal remains usable regardless of these choices.

This website

These pages do not use advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Deletion, requests and updates

You can delete journal entries from History. Uninstalling the app normally removes local app data. Delete exported backups separately from the device or storage service where you saved them. For data processed by a third-party service, see that provider’s information about available controls. To ask about this policy or exercise applicable privacy rights, use the publisher contact once it is listed on this page. When the policy changes, the updated text and date will appear here.
